About BONAVEE REAL ESTATE SERVICES

Our Story

Three Years of Construction & Real Estate Excellence

Founded by Nnamani Bonaventure chiemelie in Awka, Anambra State, BONAVEE REAL ESTATE SERVICES has grown from a specialized design-build office into a respected real estate, construction, and estate management entity.

We bridge the gap between architectural concept and structural reality. Our work spans residential estates, commercial facilities, interior upgrades, and comprehensive property asset management across Nigeria and diaspora investor networks in the US, UK, and Canada.

Our Mission

To engineer functional, sustainable, and aesthetically superior spaces while generating long-term equity for investors and property owners.

Our Vision

To become Eastern Nigeria's premier real estate development and architectural construction brand recognized globally for craftsmanship.

Core Values

Integrity, Precision Engineering, Client Confidentiality, Timely Delivery, and Innovation in Architectural Spatial Planning.
